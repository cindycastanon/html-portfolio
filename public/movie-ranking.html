Cindy's Favorite Movies!

My top 3 favorites movies at the moment.

Good Will Hunting

I really love Matt Damon's acting in this movie. Love how movie dives into problems like self-esteem, heartbreak, insecurity and much more.

10 reasons Why I Hate You

Definitely one of my favorite rom-coms.
Love these two people's relationships, how the girl at first perceives herself as a man-hater and will never get into a relationship up until she encounters a guy who makes her change her perspective in love.

Scarface

Classic movie with Al Pacino. Definitely enjoy the cuban guy with addiction issues and a need for killing.
